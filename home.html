Course Overview

Basic Toolkit for Bioinformatics Research

This website is a personal recap of the course “The Basic Toolkit for Bioinformatics Research”. It brings together the key concepts, tools, and practical skills introduced throughout the course, all in one place. Rather than being a complete manual, this site is meant to be a compact reference and a starting point for further exploration.

What Is This Website About?

Bioinformatics research is an elaborate combination of biology, computation, and critical thinking. Over the course, we explored how scientific ideas are developed, how data is analyzed and visualized, and how research is discussed and shared among groups of scientists. This website reflects that journey by combining technical tools with broader reflections about science and its research.

What You’ll Find Here

Each section of the website focuses on a different aspect of the bioinformatics workflow:

  • Research & Resources introduces essential platforms and tools for conducting your first research, including sites for finding scientific literature or biological data, as well as programs for writing and coding for finishing your first publication
  • GitHub & Gitcovers version control basics that help track, organize, and share scientific work through presented commands after initial Set-Up
  • Visualization focuses on how to design clear, honest, and informative figures, including interactive exercises to practice graph critique
  • Podcasts features selected episodes from Night Science, highlighting the creative and reflective side of scientific research

Together, these sections summarize both the practical toolkit and the mindset needed for modern bioinformatics.

Final Notes

This website is a personal snapshot that covers the most important ideas from the course. It has become my place that I can revisit to once again learn about concepts, tools, and inspirations that support thoughtful, transparent, detailed and creative research.
